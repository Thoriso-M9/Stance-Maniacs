WHO IS STANCE MANIACS

Founded in 2019, Stance Maniacs started with a simple idea, cars should reflect the people who own them. What began as a small passion project, has grown into a trusted name for vehicle styling, custom modifications, and automotive photography. We're a team of car enthusiasts, the kind who'll spend hours getting the fitment just right or waiting for the perfect light to shoot your build. From subtle tweaks to full transformations, we treat every car that comes through our doors like it's our own.
